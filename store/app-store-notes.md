# Store Notes

## Working Title

The Watcher

## Short Pitch

A phone camera horror game where blinking, looking away, or moving at the wrong time lets the entity get closer.

## First Pack

The Watcher

## Store Positioning

Short, replayable horror challenges built for phones. The camera watches your face, catches the moment you fail, and creates a shareable failure card.

## Rating Notes

The first version should avoid gore. Fear should come from suspense, jump timing, distorted overlays, and personal camera reactions.

## Privacy Positioning

Camera processing should be described clearly and simply: face tracking runs on device, caught cards are created locally, and sharing happens only after the player chooses it.
